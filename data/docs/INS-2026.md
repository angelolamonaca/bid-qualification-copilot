# Insurance certificate summary 2026

## Professional indemnity insurance

Contoso Infrastructure holds professional indemnity insurance of CHF 20 million per claim. The professional indemnity policy is issued by Helvetic Mutual Assurance and is valid from 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027. The policy covers claims arising from Contoso's professional services, including design, inspection and advisory work, in all business lines.

## Public liability insurance

Contoso Infrastructure holds public liability insurance of CHF 10 million. The public liability policy covers injury to third parties and damage to third-party property during Contoso's site work, such as inspections on roads, railways and harbors. Public liability insurance is separate from professional indemnity insurance and must not be quoted as professional indemnity cover.

## Using insurance certificates in bids

Bid managers can request copies of the 2026 insurance certificates from Finance for use in proposals. The current professional indemnity policy period ends on 31 March 2027. When a contract period extends beyond 31 March 2027, the bid manager must ask Finance to confirm the renewal position before stating the insurance cover in a proposal.
