# Reference project: Structural assessment of three rail viaducts, Northvale Rail

## Project summary

Reference project PRJ-2022-017 was the structural assessment and load rating of three masonry and concrete rail viaducts for Northvale Rail. Contoso Infrastructure completed the Northvale Rail viaduct assessment in November 2022 for a fee of CHF 210,000. The three viaducts carry a double-track main line over a river and several local roads, and the oldest structure has masonry arches dating from the nineteenth century.

## Assessment methods

The Northvale Rail viaduct assessment (PRJ-2022-017) used the following methods:

- a span-by-span condition survey of arches, piers, abutments and parapets;
- material testing of masonry, mortar and concrete cores;
- load-rating calculations for current and planned rail traffic;
- strengthening options for spans and piers below the required load rating.

## Project team

The team lead for the Northvale Rail viaduct assessment (PRJ-2022-017) was Marco Bianchi, a chartered structural engineer. Marco Bianchi coordinated the span-by-span survey, checked the load-rating calculations and presented the strengthening options to the Northvale Rail asset management team. The project team also included two structural engineers and a materials testing specialist.

## Outcome

The Northvale Rail viaduct assessment (PRJ-2022-017) confirmed that all three viaducts can remain in service. Two spans need strengthening before heavier freight trains are introduced, and Northvale Rail has added the recommended works to its renewal programme. The assessment was completed on schedule and within the agreed fee, and PRJ-2022-017 can be cited as a structural assessment reference.
