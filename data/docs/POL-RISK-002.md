# Contract Risk Review Standard

## Purpose

The Contract Risk Review Standard helps Contoso Infrastructure identify contract terms that must be escalated before a bid is registered in the CRM. The Contract Risk Review Standard defines two categories: high-risk terms, which require Regional Director approval, and prohibited terms, which prevent a GO recommendation. Bid managers must check all tender documents, including draft contract conditions, against the standard.

## High-risk terms

Under the Contract Risk Review Standard, each of the following high-risk terms requires Regional Director approval of the bid, whatever the estimated fee:

- HR-1: unlimited liability, or a liability cap above two times the fee.
- HR-2: liquidated damages above 10% of the fee.
- HR-3: transfer of Contoso's pre-existing intellectual property to the client.
- HR-4: payment terms longer than 90 days.
- HR-5: client requirements to use AI tools, or to deliver AI-generated content, that have not passed Contoso's AI review.

## Prohibited terms

Under the Contract Risk Review Standard, a bid that contains any of the following prohibited terms cannot be registered with a GO recommendation:

- PR-1: work for sanctioned entities.
- PR-2: conflict of interest — inspecting or certifying an asset Contoso designed in the last five years.

No approver, including the Regional Director, may override a prohibited term.

## How to flag a term

To flag a high-risk or prohibited contract term under the Contract Risk Review Standard, the bid manager must:

1. quote the clause, including its clause number in the tender documents;
2. name the category ID, for example HR-1 or PR-2;
3. add the category ID and the quoted clause to the CRM registration's risk flags.

The approver named in the Bid Governance Policy POL-BID-001 reviews every flagged term before deciding on the bid.
