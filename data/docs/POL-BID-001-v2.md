# Bid Governance Policy

## Purpose and scope

The Bid Governance Policy defines how Contoso Infrastructure decides which opportunities to pursue and who may approve a bid. The Bid Governance Policy applies to all bids and proposals with an estimated fee above CHF 20,000, in all business lines. Bid managers and approvers are responsible for applying the policy.

## Bid registration

Under the Bid Governance Policy, every bid must be registered in the CRM before proposal work exceeds 16 hours. A bid registration must contain the following required fields: client, opportunity title, tender reference, submission deadline, estimated fee in CHF, bid manager, go/no-go recommendation, and a short description of any known contract risks.

## Go/no-go criteria

Under the Bid Governance Policy, a bid may receive a GO recommendation only if it satisfies all of the following go/no-go criteria:

- at least two relevant reference projects completed in the last five years;
- a named team lead who meets the tender's experience requirements;
- every mandatory tender requirement met or covered by a mitigation plan;
- acceptable contract risk.

If any go/no-go criterion is not satisfied, the bid manager must recommend NO-GO.

## Approval authority

Under the Bid Governance Policy, approval authority depends on the estimated fee:

- The Business Line Lead approves bids with an estimated fee up to CHF 500,000, regardless of contract risk.
- The Regional Director approves bids with an estimated fee above CHF 500,000.

The approver reviews the go/no-go recommendation and the known contract risks before deciding, and the approval decision is recorded in the CRM against the bid registration.
