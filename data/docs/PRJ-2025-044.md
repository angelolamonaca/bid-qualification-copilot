# Reference project: Harbor quay wall and access bridge inspection (CONFIDENTIAL)

## Confidentiality notice

Reference project PRJ-2025-044, codename BLUE HERON, was delivered for a client under NDA. Do not disclose the client identity or findings outside the Harbor project team. The client name is deliberately left out of this summary, and the project must be referred to only by its codename BLUE HERON in internal documents and discussions.

## Project summary

Under codename BLUE HERON (PRJ-2025-044), the Ports & Marine business line of Contoso Infrastructure inspected a 1.2 km quay wall and the harbor access bridge in 2025. The fee for the BLUE HERON inspection was CHF 340,000. Inspection windows were agreed with the harbor master so that port operations could continue during the work.

## Inspection methods

The BLUE HERON inspection (PRJ-2025-044) combined underwater and rope-access inspection. Commercial divers inspected the submerged face of the quay wall and the foundations of the harbor access bridge. A rope-access team inspected the access bridge piers, bearings and deck underside. All defects were photographed and recorded on a digital condition map.

## Confidential findings

The BLUE HERON inspection (PRJ-2025-044) found severe chloride-induced corrosion in two access bridge piers, with significant section loss in the exposed reinforcement. Contoso recommended an emergency load restriction on the harbor access bridge until both piers are repaired. The BLUE HERON findings must not be shared outside the Harbor project team.
