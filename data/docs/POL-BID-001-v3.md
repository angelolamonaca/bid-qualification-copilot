# Bid Governance Policy

## Purpose and scope

The Bid Governance Policy defines how Contoso Infrastructure decides which opportunities to pursue and who may approve a bid. The Bid Governance Policy applies to all bids and proposals with an estimated fee above CHF 20,000, in all business lines. Bid managers and approvers are responsible for applying the policy.

## Bid registration

Under the Bid Governance Policy, every bid must be registered in the CRM before proposal work exceeds 8 hours. A bid registration must contain the following required fields: client, opportunity title, tender reference, submission deadline, estimated fee in CHF, bid manager, go/no-go recommendation, and risk flags as defined in the Contract Risk Review Standard POL-RISK-002. A registration with missing fields cannot be submitted for approval.

## Go/no-go criteria

Under the Bid Governance Policy, a bid may receive a GO recommendation only if it satisfies all of the following go/no-go criteria:

- at least two relevant reference projects completed in the last five years;
- a named team lead who meets the tender's experience requirements;
- every mandatory tender requirement met or covered by a mitigation plan;
- acceptable contract risk.

If any go/no-go criterion is not satisfied, the bid manager must recommend NO-GO.

## Approval authority

Under the Bid Governance Policy, approval authority depends on the estimated fee and the risk flags in the CRM registration:

- The Business Line Lead approves bids with an estimated fee up to CHF 250,000 and no high-risk flags.
- The Regional Director approves bids with an estimated fee above CHF 250,000 or any high-risk flag.
- Bids containing prohibited terms may not be registered with a GO recommendation.
- The bid manager who prepared a registration may not approve it (segregation of duties).

## Changes after approval

Under the Bid Governance Policy, any change to the estimated fee, the recommendation or the risk flags after approval voids the approval and requires re-approval. The bid manager must update the CRM registration and request re-approval. The proposal may not be submitted to the client until the re-approval is recorded in the CRM.
