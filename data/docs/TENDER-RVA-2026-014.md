# Request for Proposal: Condition inspection of 12 road bridges

## Overview

The River Valley Road Authority invites proposals for the condition inspection of 12 road bridges built between 1965 and 1998 in the River Valley district. The consultant will assess each bridge and recommend maintenance measures. The contract period is January to November 2027.

## Submission

The submission deadline for River Valley Road Authority tender reference RVA-2026-014 is 30 October 2026, 12:00 CET. Proposals must be uploaded as PDF files via the Authority's e-tender portal. Late proposals and proposals sent by email or post will not be considered.

## Mandatory requirements

Bidders for tender RVA-2026-014 must meet all of the following mandatory requirements. Non-compliant proposals will be excluded.

- R1: at least two bridge inspection or structural assessment references completed in the last five years.
- R2: a team lead with at least 10 years of bridge engineering experience.
- R3: valid ISO 45001 certification (occupational health and safety).
- R4: professional indemnity insurance of at least CHF 10 million.
- R5: capability for rope-access inspection of bridge undersides.
- R6: experience installing weigh-in-motion (WIM) sensors on bridges.

## Budget

The River Valley Road Authority's budget estimate for tender RVA-2026-014 is CHF 150,000–220,000 excluding VAT. The budget estimate is indicative only. Bidders must state their own fee in CHF, excluding VAT. Fees are evaluated together with the technical quality of each proposal.

## Contract terms

The draft contract for tender RVA-2026-014 includes the following terms:

- Clause 14.2: "The Consultant's liability under this contract shall be unlimited."
- Clause 15.1: liquidated damages of 0.5% of the fee per week of delay, capped at 5% of the fee.
- Clause 18: payment within 60 days of invoice.
- Clause 21: all inspection reports must be drafted with the Authority's "InspectAI" reporting assistant.
