# Reference project: Bridge inspection programme, Lakeside Canton

## Project summary

Reference project PRJ-2024-031 was the condition inspection of 18 road bridges for the Lakeside Canton Roads Department. Contoso Infrastructure delivered the Lakeside Canton bridge inspection programme from March 2023 to November 2024 for a fee of CHF 290,000. The bridges in the programme are concrete, steel and composite structures on the cantonal road network.

## Inspection methods

The Lakeside Canton bridge inspection programme (PRJ-2024-031) used the following methods:

- visual and hands-on inspection of all 18 bridges;
- a rope-access team for bridge undersides, bearings and expansion joints;
- drone photogrammetry of decks, piers and abutments;
- condition ratings and maintenance recommendations for every bridge.

## Project team

The team lead for the Lakeside Canton bridge inspection programme (PRJ-2024-031) was Marco Bianchi (Senior Bridge Engineer). Marco Bianchi planned the inspections, supervised the rope-access team, reviewed all condition ratings and presented the results to the Lakeside Canton Roads Department. The project team also included three bridge engineers and a drone pilot.

## Outcome

The Lakeside Canton bridge inspection programme (PRJ-2024-031) was delivered on time, and client satisfaction was rated 4.6/5. Following the programme, the Lakeside Canton Roads Department awarded Contoso a follow-on maintenance design contract. PRJ-2024-031 can be cited as a reference for bridge inspection and structural assessment tenders.
