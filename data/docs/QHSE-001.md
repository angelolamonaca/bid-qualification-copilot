# Management system certifications

## Quality and environmental certifications

Contoso Infrastructure holds ISO 9001:2015 certification for its quality management system and ISO 14001:2015 certification for its environmental management system. Both certifications are valid until 2028 and cover all offices and business lines. An accredited certification body carries out annual surveillance audits of the ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 management systems.

## Occupational health and safety management

Occupational health and safety at Contoso Infrastructure follows an internal management system. The internal occupational health and safety system covers risk assessments, method statements for site work, rope-access and working-at-height procedures, incident reporting and annual safety training. The QHSE team audits the internal system every year, but the system is not externally certified.

## ISO 45001 certification status

Contoso Infrastructure does not currently hold ISO 45001 certification for occupational health and safety. ISO 45001 certification is planned for Q2 2027 and is not yet held. Until the ISO 45001 certificate has been issued, bid managers must not state or imply in any proposal that Contoso is ISO 45001 certified. QHSE reports progress towards ISO 45001 certification to the executive team every quarter.
