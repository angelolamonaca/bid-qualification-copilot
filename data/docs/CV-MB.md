# CV summary: Marco Bianchi, Senior Bridge Engineer

## Profile

Marco Bianchi is a Senior Bridge Engineer in the Transportation business line of Contoso Infrastructure, with 14 years of bridge engineering experience. Marco Bianchi specialises in condition inspection, structural assessment and load rating of existing bridges and viaducts, and regularly leads inspection teams for road and rail clients.

## Qualifications

Marco Bianchi is a chartered structural engineer and holds an MSc in civil engineering. Marco Bianchi is certified at IRATA rope-access Level 2, which allows him to carry out hands-on inspections of bridge undersides and other hard-to-reach structural elements as part of a supervised rope-access team.

## Reference projects

Marco Bianchi was team lead on the following Contoso reference projects:

- PRJ-2024-031 (2023–2024): condition inspection of 18 road bridges for the Lakeside Canton Roads Department, including rope-access inspection of bridge undersides.
- PRJ-2022-017 (2022): structural assessment and load rating of three rail viaducts for Northvale Rail.

## Availability

Marco Bianchi's availability for new assignments is 60% from January 2027. The rest of Marco Bianchi's time is committed to existing project work. Requests to name Marco Bianchi as team lead in a proposal must be agreed with the Transportation business line before the proposal is submitted.
